# REso - 93-Day Resolutions Tracker

A focused, minimal, dark-rose resolution and habit tracking application designed for daily consistency and accountability from **1 Oct 2026 to 1 Jan 2027 (93 days)**.

---

## Features

- **Core Resolutions Tracking:**
  - **Outside Food:** Clean home meals vs. restaurant / outside food.
  - **11:30 PM Sleep Boundary:** On-time sleep vs. late scrolling.
  - **Khud Pe Focus:** Prioritizing mental and physical peace vs. neglected/stressed.
  - **Family & Myself:** Quality time spent, busy days, self-care recovery, or disconnected.
  - **Gym & Movement:** Workouts, body recovery rest days, or skipped (optional toggle).
- **Health & Cycle Integration:**
  - **Period & Unwell Tracking:** Automatically suggests self-care rest for gym and family/self recovery, while keeping workouts fully editable if completed.
- **Occasional Boundaries:**
  - Situational boundary check with tactile toggles: *Given explanation*, *Given sympathy*, and *Exaggerated thing*.
- **Accurate Calendar & Months Overview:**
  - Clean day dots with color-coding: Green (Clean), Sky Blue (Rest/Busy), Red (Missed), Hot Pink (Period), Amber (Unwell).
  - Upcoming dates are never pre-counted in advance - counts strictly reflect elapsed/completed days.
- **Real Reasons & Detailed Exceptions Report:**
  - Replaces vague labels with exact real reasons (e.g. *2 days ate outside food*, *1 day past 11:30 PM*).
  - Day-by-day exception timeline highlighting what happened and why.

## Running Locally

```bash
# Start local live-reload server (Node.js)
node server.js
```

Open `http://localhost:3000` in any modern browser.
